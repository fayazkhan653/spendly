Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account and use the service, including:

  • Account Information: Email address and password.
  • Financial Data: Expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions that you manually input.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our application.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the expense tracking service.
  • To generate spending reports and visualizations for your account.
  • To improve the functionality and user experience of Spendly.
  • To communicate with you regarding your account or updates to the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal and financial information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We may use third-party hosting providers to store data, but these providers are prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than providing services to us.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
